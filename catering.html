2. Key Technical Infrastructure & Special Features

  • Provision of LCD Display Screens: There is provision of LCD display screens throughout the Centre providing a facility for daily program information, special announcements, PowerPoint presentations, promotional footage and even sponsor advertising.
  • In-Floor Service Pits: The Exhibition Hall features service pits installed in the floor to provide power, data, telephone lines.
  • Corporate & Conference Branding Theming: There are many additional opportunities to theme the entire Centre with corporate or conference branding, and have a dynamic impact on your event attendees.
  • Power Infrastructure: 100% Uninterrupted DG Power Backup with dual 250kVA generators.
  • Stage Dimensions: 60 ft (Width) × 25 ft (Depth) with 24 ft clear vertical ceiling clearance.
  • Parking Concourse: On-site paved parking for 500+ four-wheelers and 1,000+ two-wheelers with valet drivers.
  • Kitchens: Dual segregated kitchens for Pure Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian food preparation.
